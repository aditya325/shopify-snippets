## Context

Neither component exists today — the current footer only lists `cart.cart_level_discount_applications`. 0.1 provides `recommended_products` and `recommended_heading`. This theme's money-formatting convention (CLAUDE.md) requires stripping the currency-format space and trailing zeroes on every displayed price; the cart surfaces don't do this yet.

## Goal

A 4-row price summary and a merchant-curated recommended-products rail, both reading real cart/product data, both following this theme's money-formatting convention.

## Requirements

### Functional
- Price summary (§5.9): exactly 4 rows — Total MRP, Discount On MRP, Coupon Discount, Total Amount. No Delivery Fee row (drawn in the file, removed by decision)
- Recommended products (§5.10): 376×301 container, radius 12, `#466E2E` stroke, 4 cards from `recommended_products`, each with category label + title + a "Buy" control that adds to cart without closing the drawer
- All prices render with no decimals and no trailing zeroes

## Approach

Two independent snippets: `snippets/cart-price-summary.liquid` (pure Liquid math off `cart.original_total_price`/`cart.total_price`/discount allocations) and `snippets/cart-recommended-products.liquid` (loops `recommended_products`, reuses the add-to-cart pattern already in `snippets/add-to-cart-button.liquid`). The card control's label ("Buy" here, "Add to Cart" in 3.1's empty state) is a render param, not two snippets. Apply `| money_without_trailing_zeros | replace: ' ', ''` per CLAUDE.md's money convention on every price in both snippets.

## Expected Output

- `snippets/cart-price-summary.liquid` — new
- `snippets/cart-recommended-products.liquid` — new
