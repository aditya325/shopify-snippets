## Context

Sprint §4.7. The Subscribe & Save band — email capture plus a row of USP blocks, on a
three-tier background. This is the first section to consume the newsletter snippet extracted in
1.4, and it cannot be built before that lands.

Background priority is a **three-way** rule, not the usual two: video over image over gradient.
Video and image each have separate desktop and mobile fields, so the section carries five
background fields plus the gradient.

Type note from Sprint §2.1: the heading is Space Grotesk Medium 32 desktop in Figma and
normalizes to **Bold**. The extraction records it at w600 SemiBold — either way it renders Bold
per the normalization rule, which applies to headings.

## Goal

A Subscribe & Save band with a three-tier background, merchant-controlled USP blocks, and the
shared newsletter form.

## Requirements

### Functional
- Section-level: title (`richtext`, underline-enabled), logo (`image_picker`), subline (`text`), background gradient (`color_background`), background image desktop + mobile (`image_picker` ×2), background video desktop + mobile (`video` ×2)
- **Background priority: video > image > gradient**, evaluated per viewport
- Block type `usp` with: icon (`image_picker`), title (`richtext`), description (`text`)
- Email capture renders `snippets/newsletter-form.liquid` from 1.4
- Measured padding: desktop 32 bottom / 60 sides; mobile 24 bottom / 10 sides

> **This section paints a background, so it takes BOTH:** `margin` on the wrapper for its separation from neighbours, and `padding` on the element carrying the background for the painted interior. Margin falls outside the painted box, so without the padding the band collapses to the content height (CLAUDE.md §5).
- `presets` with four USP blocks; no `max_blocks`

### Non-Functional
- Background video muted, `playsinline`, looped, lazy-initialised near viewport, poster from `video.preview_image`
- Background images as `<picture>` with separate desktop and mobile sources
- Text contrast must hold against all three background tiers

## Approach

The three-way background fallback is the main logic. Resolve it once in a `{%- liquid -%}` block
at the top of the section — check video first, then image, then fall through to the gradient —
rather than nesting conditionals through the markup. Because desktop and mobile have separate
video and image fields, the resolution happens per viewport: a merchant may set a desktop video
and only a mobile image, and both must work.

For the video background, do not use a CSS background — Global Standards §7 forbids it. Use a
positioned `<video>` element with `object-fit: cover` behind the content, same as the image
treatment.

The newsletter form comes from 1.4 with the subscribe-letter style variant. Do not re-implement
the form, and do not copy markup from `sections/newsletter.liquid`.

The USP row shows four items in a 2×2 arrangement on mobile per the extraction (two `Service
Item` columns each containing two `Service Container` rows). Confirm against the screenshot and
match; do not assume a 4-across row collapses to a single column.

Take the exact gradient stops, icon container sizes and per-element type from the "Subscribe &
Save" tables in `design-context.md`.
