## Context

Sprint §4.10. Curated wellness bundles — products from a single collection rendered through the
1.5 product-card snippet, on a `#466E2E33` tinted background.

Two behaviours distinguish it from 2.11. First, **price is hidden here by section toggle** —
the design deliberately omits price in this placement, so the card's `hide_price` flag is
exercised for the first time. Second, the carousel rule is conditional on product count:
**exactly 4 render statically; more than 4 enables Embla at 4.2 slides per view on desktop with
dots; mobile is always a dotted carousel.**

## Goal

A single-collection product section on a tinted background, static at 4 products, a 4.2-per-view
carousel beyond that on desktop, always a carousel on mobile, with price hidden by default.

## Requirements

### Functional
- Section-level: heading (`richtext`, underline-enabled), collection (`collection`), show price (`checkbox`, **off** per design), show product featured image in cards (`checkbox`), background colour (`color`)
- Renders products through `snippets/product-card.liquid`, passing `hide_price` and `show_featured_image`
- Desktop: exactly 4 products render statically; >4 enables Embla at **4.2 slides per view** with dots
- Mobile: always a dotted Embla carousel
- Measured padding: desktop 32 vertical / 60 sides, gap 24; mobile 24 vertical / 20 sides, gap 20

> **This section paints a background, so it takes BOTH:** `margin` on the wrapper for its separation from neighbours, and `padding` on the element carrying the background for the painted interior. Margin falls outside the painted box, so without the padding the band collapses to the content height (CLAUDE.md §5).
- `presets`; no `max_blocks` (product count comes from the collection, not blocks)

### Non-Functional
- Below the fold — card images lazy + `decoding="async"`
- Dots keyboard-operable with accessible labels

## Approach

This section and 2.6 are the two consumers of 0.6's conditional-on-count activation, but the
threshold differs (4 here, 3 there) and the counted thing differs — **product count from the
collection, not block count**. Compute it in Liquid from `collection.products_count` (or the
rendered product count after any limit) and emit it as a data attribute for 0.6 to act on.

Note the count that matters is how many products actually render, not how many the collection
holds. If the section caps at some number, the cap is what drives the carousel decision. Sprint
§4.10 says "exactly 4 rendered statically; more than 4 enables Embla" — so there is no cap and
all products render. Confirm that reading against the design before implementing a limit.

The desktop static-to-carousel switch plus the mobile always-on carousel means a
breakpoint-conditional **and** count-conditional activation at once. Verify the teardown case:
at exactly 4 products, crossing from mobile to desktop must fully destroy the mobile carousel.

`hide_price` is a new path through 1.5 that no earlier section exercises — check that hiding the
price does not leave a gap where the price row was, and that the card's vertical rhythm still
matches the design.

Background is a section-level `color` setting defaulting to the measured `#466E2E33`; apply it
inline since it is merchant-controlled.

## Constraints

- Price hidden by default in this placement (Sprint §4.10)
- Owns a complete standalone JS file using the Embla globals vendored by 0.6. No shared module, base class or helper — duplication across the five carousel sections is accepted by decision. Boots on `DOMContentLoaded`
- Product data via 1.5 metafields, never hardcoded
