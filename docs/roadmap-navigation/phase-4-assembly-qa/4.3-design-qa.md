## Context

The final gate. Every panel is built and populated; this PRD compares the live header against all 17
Figma frames at 390 and 1440, and fixes what does not match.

Run `/compare` against the extraction rather than eyeballing. `sections.json` maps each canonical
section name to its screenshot pair and already corrects the Figma layer names — three frames in this
section are misnamed (`389:30270` and `389:30486` are labelled "About - History - Mobile" but render
Media; the desktop header instance is labelled "Mobile Menu").

> ⛔ **The TDD is frozen.** `docs/roadmap-navigation/deviations.md` is the authority here, not the
> TDD. Where the two disagree, `deviations.md` wins. Read it in full before starting.

## Goal

Every panel matches its Figma frame at both viewports, with each remaining difference either fixed
or recorded in `deviations.md` as a deliberate, justified departure.

## Requirements

### Functional
- Compare the **8 sections with desktop frames** at 1440, and the **9 with mobile frames** at 390 — 17 comparisons. `categories-expanded` and `drawer-root` are mobile-only; `about-history` is desktop-only
- Verify the measurements this sprint resolved rather than trusting them: nav label 18/27, mobile bar **66px**, drawer row **52px**, chip fill `#fde047`, utility strip `padding 16/64` with a `0.5px #a3a3a3` top border
- Verify panel heights: Categories 495, Media 573 (both), About 573, Combo 605, Shop By Need 637, About History 668
- Verify the approved deviations render as intended and are **not** logged as defects

### Non-Functional
- Every difference classified: fixed, accepted deviation, or design error — no unclassified diffs

## Approach

Six entries in `deviations.md` are design-visible and must not be raised as defects. List them at the
top of the QA record before starting:

1. **D-6** — the account icon ships though the design omits it
2. **D-13** — chevrons ship in the scrolled state though `389:29163` shows plain labels
3. **D-2** — the mobile header is 66px, not the 59 + 66 in Sprint §2.2
4. **D-3** — the drawer row is 52px, not 48
5. **D-4** — the chip is `#fde047` fill with no border, not a `#EAB308` border
6. **D-7** — About → History has no mobile frame; the drawer rendering is unverified, not wrong

**`panel-border #A3A3A3` is the one measurement this sprint could not confirm.** Sprint §2.2 calls it
the outer border of the dropdown container, but extraction only found it as a `0.5px` top border on
the utility strip. Measure the dropdown container in Figma during this pass; if the border does not
exist, record it as a new entry in `deviations.md` rather than editing §2.2.

**F3's animation timing** (250ms `ease-out`, chosen as a house default in 2.3) needs designer
sign-off here — it is the one value in this sprint with no design source.

Where a fix would break a value another PRD verified, record the conflict rather than picking a side
unilaterally.
