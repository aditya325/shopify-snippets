# PRD: 4.2 — Accessibility and Interaction Pass

**Phase:** Phase 4 — Assembly & QA
**Priority:** P0
**Depends on:** 2.3 panel behaviour, 2.4 mobile drawer, 4.1 editor wiring
**Estimated scope:** Medium (audit + fixes across the header files)

## Context

A mega menu is the hardest component in a theme to get right for keyboard and screen-reader users,
and Sprint §4.1 acknowledges it directly — hover alone is unreachable, so click and focus
equivalents are required. 2.3 and 2.4 built those equivalents; this PRD audits them against the whole
populated header and fixes what fails.

**Two known gaps carry over from the homepage sprint** and are worth checking rather than assuming.
`docs/roadmap-homepage/phase-1-shared-components/1.2-button-snippet.md` records that a **44×44 touch target
was a stated criterion that never shipped**, and its focus outline arrived only in an amendment. The
header's 18px chevrons and 24px mobile icons are prime candidates for the same failure.

> ⛔ **The TDD is frozen.** Never edit `docs/tdds/amrutanjan-navigation-sprint.md`. Accepted
> shortfalls are recorded in `deviations.md`, never dropped silently.

## Goal

The whole header — bar, five panels, mobile drawer — is operable by keyboard, screen reader and
touch, with no trap, no unreachable content and no unlabelled control.

## Requirements

### Functional
- Every top-level trigger reachable by `Tab` in visual order; `Enter`/`Space` opens, `Escape` closes and restores focus
- An open panel's rail rows and pane content are in the tab order; closed panels are fully removed from it
- `aria-expanded` on every trigger tracks real state; `aria-haspopup` only where a panel exists
- The mobile drawer traps focus while open, restores it to the hamburger on close, and its level-2 push does not strand focus on the hidden level
- All interactive controls have accessible names — search, cart, account, close, back, hamburger
- Interactive targets meet **44×44 CSS pixels**, including the 18px chevrons and 24px mobile icons
- Visible focus indicator on every interactive element in **both** header states
- `prefers-reduced-motion` honoured by the panel animation, the drawer transition and the sticky background change

### Non-Functional
- Colour contrast meets WCAG AA: check `#466e2e` on white, `#171717` on `#faf9f6`, and white on the subscribe gradient's lightest stop
- Screen-reader pass on at least one desktop and one mobile combination

## Approach

**Audit before fixing.** Run a keyboard-only pass through all five panels and both drawer levels,
then a screen-reader pass, and record every failure before changing code — otherwise fixes chase
each other.

Touch targets are the most likely systematic failure. The design's 18px chevron and 24px icons are
**visual** sizes; the hit area must be expanded independently via padding or a pseudo-element,
without changing the rendered glyph size or the 40px nav gap. Do not scale the icons up to pass.

The subscribe promo's gradient is the contrast risk worth measuring rather than eyeballing: white
and `rgba(255,255,255,.6)` text sits over stops ranging from `#f4c430` to `#466e2e`, and the lightest
end is where AA is likely to fail.

Where a criterion cannot be met without breaking the design, **record it as an accepted deviation in
`deviations.md`** rather than silently dropping it — that is exactly what happened to the 44×44
target on the homepage sprint and it should not repeat unnoticed.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Full keyboard pass through all 5 panels and both drawer levels with no trap and no unreachable content
- [ ] `Escape` closes any open panel or the drawer and restores focus to the correct trigger
- [ ] `aria-expanded` verified correct on all 5 chevroned triggers; absent on Offers and Track Order
- [ ] Closed panels are absent from the tab order
- [ ] Every icon-only control has an accessible name, verified in an accessibility tree dump
- [ ] Every interactive target measures ≥44×44 CSS px, **or** the shortfall is recorded in `deviations.md`
- [ ] Visible focus indicator present on all controls in both resting and scrolled states
- [ ] `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` disables the panel, drawer and background transitions
- [ ] Contrast measured and passing AA, including white text on the gradient's lightest stop
- [ ] Screen-reader pass completed and recorded on ≥1 desktop and ≥1 mobile combination
- [ ] `docs/tdds/amrutanjan-navigation-sprint.md` is unchanged
