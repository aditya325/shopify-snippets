## Goal

The Media panel renders three blog cards or three press cards depending on the rail row, each beside
the Subscribe & Save promo, on both viewports.

## Requirements

### press-card
- Takes an `article` object; image on top, then a meta row, headline and Read More link
- Meta row: publisher green left, published date grey right, on one line
- Publisher from `article.metafields.custom.publisher`; **hidden when unset**, without the date losing its right alignment
- Date derived from `article.published_at`, in the design's "Aug 30" short form
- Headline wraps to 2–3 lines; card heights stay equal across a row
- "Read More ›" link to the article
- **No category badge** — this is what distinguishes it from `article-card`
- Whole card clickable via a stretched link on the headline; exactly one anchor in the DOM
- An article with no featured image renders without a broken image or a collapsed card

### Panel
- Rail: 2 rows, no icons — Blogs, News & Events
- **Blogs pane:** heading "Blogs"; 3 portrait cards via `article-card` with a supplied `read_label`; full-width "Check All Blogs" button
- **News pane:** heading "News"; 3 cards via `press-card`; full-width "Check All News & Events" button
- Counts from `articles_count` / `press_count`, default 3
- Both panes 684px with the 352px promo at `x:1028`
- CTA falls back to the blog's URL when `cta_link` is empty
- Mobile Blogs: **2-up** cards then the CTA. Mobile News: cards stack full width, copy below the image

## Approach

**Read `snippets/article-card.liquid` first and follow its conventions.** Two are load-bearing and
documented in its own doc block: the stretched-link pattern, where the read affordance is a `span`
and never an anchor so the card stays a single anchor; and the `tracking-normal` reset on the root,
which exists because `base.css` sets `body { letter-spacing: 0.01em }` and it inherits as a flat
0.16px into every child regardless of its own size.

**Do not modify `article-card`.** Sprint §6 requires it unchanged, and it ships to the homepage
`featured-blogs` section. It requires a caller-supplied `read_label` with no default — expose it as
a rail-level setting rather than hardcoding "Read" or defaulting it inside the card.

Date formatting uses Liquid's `date` filter. The design shows "Aug 30" — short month plus day, no
year. Check the store's locale files rather than hardcoding an English month name.

The publisher-unset case matters: the meta row is a two-item `justify-between`, so with the
publisher gone the date must stay right-aligned rather than sliding left. Use a spacer or a grid.

One pane snippet with a branch, not two — the panes share a frame and differ only in which card they
render. But do **not** normalise the two card heights: Blogs cards are portrait with an overlaid
title, press cards landscape with copy beneath, and they are meant to look different.

Mobile diverges between the two panes — Blogs 2-up, News stacked full width. Build both explicitly.
